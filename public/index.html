About the club

Free, always

Tennis shouldn't cost a fortune to try. Our sessions are free so anyone can get on court.

Beginner friendly

Never held a racket? Perfect. Players of every level hit together, and someone's always happy to show you the basics.

Community first

Tennis is the excuse; the community is the point. Expect music, good vibes, and new friends.

How it works

  1. Join the group chat

    That's where we post session times, court locations, and last-minute updates.

  2. Pull up on Friday nights

    Our regular Friday night sessions are the easiest way to get started. Bring water and comfy shoes.

  3. Play, rotate, repeat

    We rotate partners so everyone gets court time and meets new people. Spare rackets are often around. Just ask.

FAQ

Is it really free?

Yes. Our regular sessions are free. Some special events with partners may have their own RSVP, but we'll always say so up front.

I've never played. Can I still come?

Absolutely. The club was made for beginners. You'll be hitting with people at every level.

Do I need my own racket?

Bring one if you have it. If not, let us know in the group chat and we'll try to have a spare.

Where do we play?

Court locations are shared in the group chat before each session.
