Tennis is the excuse. Community is the point.

BK Tennis Club makes tennis accessible to anyone who wants to try it, whether you've played for years or never held a racket.

Free, always

Tennis shouldn't cost a fortune to try. Our sessions are free so anyone can get on court.

Beginner friendly

Never played? Perfect. Every level hits together, and someone's always happy to show you the basics.

Community first

Music, good vibes and new friends. People come for the tennis and stay for each other.

How it works

Your first Friday night

  1. Sign up for texts

    Session times, court locations and last-minute updates come straight to your phone.

  2. Pull up on Friday

    Our regular Friday night session is the easiest way in. Bring water and comfy shoes, nothing else required.

  3. Play, rotate, repeat

    We rotate partners so everyone gets court time and meets someone new.

Questions

Is it really free?

Yes. Regular sessions are free. Partner events may have their own RSVP, and we'll always say so up front.

I've never played. Can I still come?

Absolutely. The club was made for beginners.

Do I need my own racket?

Bring one if you have it. If not, reply to one of our texts and we'll try to have a spare.

Where do we play?

Court locations are texted to the Friday Nights group before each session.
