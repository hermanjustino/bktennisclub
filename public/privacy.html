Privacy Policy

What we collect

When you sign up for texts we collect your name, mobile number, the groups you choose, and a record of your consent (the time, the wording you agreed to, and your IP address). We also keep the replies you send to our texts.

How we use it

Only to send you the club texts you signed up for, to answer your replies, and to keep records of consent and opt-outs.

Sharing

We do not sell, rent or share your mobile number or SMS consent with third parties or affiliates for marketing or promotional purposes. Text messaging originator opt-in data and consent will not be shared with any third parties.

We use service providers to run the program: Twilio sends and receives the texts, and Google Cloud hosts this site and stores sign-up records. They process your information only to provide those services to us.

Keeping and deleting your information

We keep your information while you're subscribed. If you reply STOP we stop texting you and keep a record of the opt-out so we don't text you again by mistake. To have your information deleted, message @bktennisclub on Instagram.

Changes

If we change this policy we'll update the date at the top of this page.
