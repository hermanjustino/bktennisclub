SMS Terms

The program

BK Tennis Club sends text messages about club sessions, court locations, schedule changes and events to people who sign up at bktennisclub.com. You choose which groups to join when you sign up.

Signing up

After you submit the sign-up form, we text you once to confirm. You're only added after you reply YES. Consent to receive texts is not a condition of joining sessions or anything else.

How often

Message frequency varies, usually a few messages a week depending on the groups you join.

Costs

Message and data rates may apply, according to your mobile plan. BK Tennis Club doesn't charge for texts.

Opting out and help

Reply STOP to any message to stop receiving texts. You'll get one final message confirming you've opted out. Reply START to opt back in. Reply HELP for help, or message @bktennisclub on Instagram.

Carriers

Carriers are not liable for delayed or undelivered messages.

Privacy

See our Privacy Policy for how we handle your information.
